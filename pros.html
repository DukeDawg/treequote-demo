Crew FAQ

The fine print, up front

What does it cost to join?

Nothing to join and nothing monthly. TreeQuote earns a flat referral percentage on jobs you complete, agreed before you accept your first job.

Who sets the price?

You do. The homeowner sees a ballpark range up front so expectations are realistic, then gets your firm quote built on the arborist's written scope.

Do I have to take every job?

No. Accept the jobs that fit your crew and schedule, pass on the rest. Passing never counts against you.

What if the job on site doesn't match the scope?

Send a photo and a change note through the app. The homeowner approves the change before any extra work, so nobody argues about price on the driveway.
