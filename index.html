What moves the price

  • Height and trunk size. Bigger trees take more climbing, rigging, and hauling.
  • What's underneath. Roofs, fences, and power lines mean pieces get lowered by rope, not dropped.
  • Access. Backyard-only or a steep slope can rule out a bucket truck.
  • Condition. Dead wood is brittle and riskier to climb.
  • Timing. Emergency work runs higher. Flexible dates can save you money.

Start a request
Is TreeQuote free for homeowners?

Yes. The crew pays TreeQuote a referral fee out of the job. That fee never gets added on top of the price you approved.

How accurate is the online estimate?

It's a ballpark built from your answers and photos, and it tightens as you add detail. After an arborist reviews it, you get a firm quote from the crew. Big or unusual jobs get a free site visit first.

Who are the crews?

Local tree companies we've checked for insurance, equipment, safety record, and quality of work. Each job goes to a crew that's equipped for it — a bucket-truck crew for the roadside oak, climbers for the backyard maple.

A tree just came down. Can you help today?

Choose “Storm damage” when you start your request. Emergency jobs jump the line and go to the nearest available crew. If anyone is hurt or a power line is down, call 911 and your utility first.

Who gets my phone number?

Only TreeQuote, and then the one crew you approve. We never sell or share your information with other companies.

Do I need to be home?

Not for most jobs. As long as the crew can reach the tree, you'll get photos and a text when the work is done.
